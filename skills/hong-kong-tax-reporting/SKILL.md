# Hong Kong tax reporting and filing — year of assessment 2025/26

Read `references/reporting-workflow.md` for the general method. This file covers
what is specific to Hong Kong.

## Get the calendar

```bash
python -m taxcalc deadlines HK
```

## The return comes to you

BIR60 returns are issued in early May for the year ended 31 March, with **one
month** to file — extended by a further month for electronic filing, and to
three months where the taxpayer has a sole proprietorship.

If you have chargeable income and **have not received a return**, you must
notify the IRD within four months of the end of the year of assessment — by 31
July. This obligation catches new arrivals who assume no return means no
liability.

## Provisional tax and holding it over

Each assessment includes provisional tax for the current year, based on the year
just ended. It is paid in two instalments, roughly 75% in January and 25% in April.

You can apply to **hold over** provisional tax if income has fallen by more than
10%, if you have ceased employment, or in other defined circumstances. The
application must be made no later than 28 days before the instalment is due, or
14 days after the demand — whichever is later.

This is the single most useful procedural thing to know in Hong Kong tax.
Someone who has left a job, gone part-time or taken a pay cut is otherwise
paying provisional tax on income they will not earn.

## Employer forms mark the boundaries

- **IR56E** when you start
- **IR56B** annually
- **IR56G** at least one month before you leave Hong Kong

IR56G triggers an obligation on the employer to **withhold your final payments**
until the IRD clears you. Anyone leaving Hong Kong should expect their last
salary and any accrued benefits to be held, and should file the resulting
assessment promptly to release them.

## Objections

An objection must be lodged within **one month** of the assessment date, in
writing, stating the grounds. This is short. If an assessment looks wrong,
acting quickly matters more than getting the argument perfect first time.

## Deductions to claim

Home loan interest, MPF, approved charitable donations, elderly residential care
expenses, self-education expenses, and the voluntary health insurance scheme.
Personal assessment can be elected where there is rental or business income and
it produces a lower result.

## Penalties

A fine up to 10,000 plus additional tax of up to three times the amount
undercharged for late or incorrect filing. Late payment carries a 5% surcharge,
with a further 10% if still unpaid after six months. The IRD generally accepts
reasonable explanations for a first late filing.
